Setup

Describe the subject and connect to ComfyUI. Settings are saved in project.yaml in the project folder.

ComfyUI

Sources

Add the photos you have. Mark 2–4 clear ones as Reference: they are sent to the model with every take to hold the identity. Sources marked In dataset are also used as training images. Have no photos? Go to the shot list and add an Anchor take to create the character from the description.

Drop photos here or click to choose files (JPG, PNG, WebP)

Shot list

Each row is one image you want in the training set. Vary framing, angle, expression, outfit, setting and lighting so the LoRA learns the person rather than the background. Click a row's ▸ to see the exact prompt or write your own.

Generate & pick

Generate variants for each take, then pick the one that looks most like the subject. If one is close, open it and Refine it: describe what to change and it is edited from that image. If none is close, add to the prompt in the box under the take and generate again. Click an image to compare it with the references; keys: ← → move, P pick, Esc close.

Export

Writes export/dataset/ (images with matching .txt captions), an ai-toolkit config for Qwen-Image-2.1 and a zip to upload to the training pod.
